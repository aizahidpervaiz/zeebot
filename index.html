Install in three steps

  1. Download the installer

    Click Download for Windows. Your browser saves ZeeBOT.Setup.1.0.0.exe (161 MB) to your Downloads folder. Open it when it finishes.

  2. Get past the blue Windows warning

    ZeeBOT is new and not yet signed with a paid certificate, so Windows SmartScreen shows a blue box saying Windows protected your PC. This is normal for new apps.

    Windows protected your PC
    Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting. Running this app might put your PC at risk.

  3. Finish setup and open ZeeBOT

    The installer puts ZeeBOT on your PC and adds a shortcut. From then on it works with no internet connection at all.

Totally offline

Once installed, ZeeBOT never needs the internet.

  • Your questions stay on your PC.
  • Folders you open in ZeeBOT are read on your PC and never uploaded.
  • No account, no sign-in, no tracking.

Questions

My antivirus flagged the installer. Is it safe?

Some antivirus programs are cautious about any new, unsigned app. If yours blocks it, choose its option to allow or restore the file, then run it again.

How do I update?

Come back here and download again. The button always gives you the newest version.

Does it cost anything?

No. ZeeBOT is free to download and use.
